# Sound Credits & License (`CC0 1.0` Public Domain)

All **23** Vorbis `.ogg` audio files bundled in `assets/just_a_rifle/sounds/` are
**CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain)** works — combining **Navaro** and **rzen1th**'s
Public Domain firearm/foley recordings (remastered at `48 kHz` and converted to
single-channel Mono wherever 3D `OpenAL` spatial attenuation is active) with
original first-party procedural acoustic layers created for **Just a Rifle** and
released under `CC0 1.0` / `MIT`.
